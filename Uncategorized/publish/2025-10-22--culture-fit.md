**Giới thiệu sơ qua về bản thân**

Hello, my name is Mai Tran Thuyet.

I graduated in Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

During my studies, I focused on embedded systems programming, especially how to integrate hardware and software in IoT devices.

Besides that, I’ve also been interested in AI on embedded devices, such as deploying AI models that can run efficiently on edge hardware.

I’m now looking for opportunities to combine both areas — embedded systems and AI — and I believe Asilla is a great place to grow in that direction.

**Điểm mạnh, điểm yếu của em là gì?**

My strength is my discipline (kỷ luật), careful and attention to detail.

At FPT, I had the opportunity to work on projects directly with Panasonic clients, The software development process was very structured and professional.

I learned how to work in a big system, following the V-Model process — from requirements and design to coding, review, and testing.

This experience helped me build a mindset of precision, honesty, and respect for process, which I believe fits very well with the working culture in Japanese companies.

Another one of my strengths is self-learning.

In university, the textbooks were very dense, and class time alone was not enough.

So I often studied by myself, read extra materials, and practiced at home.

Thanks to that, I developed the habit of learning independently and finding solutions on my own — a skill that helps me a lot now when I face new problem

My weakness, however, is that working in an outsourcing model, I didn’t always have a holistic view of the product, and couldn’t see how my code was used in the final system.

That’s why I now want to move to a product-focused environment, where I can better understand the full product lifecycle and contribute to the design and improvement of the actual product.

**Tại sao bạn lại nghỉ FPT?**

The main reason I decided to make a change is related to my career goal.

At FPT, I had valuable experience working on multiple projects for Japanese clients.

I learned a lot about structured software development processes and quality management, especially following the V-Model.

However, because FPT mainly focuses on outsourcing, project assignments often change, and project lifecycles can be quite short.

While this helped me gain broad experience, I realized that I wanted to work more deeply on a single product, understand its full lifecycle, and contribute to its long-term growth.

Therefore, I’m looking for a product-based environment where I can focus more on technology and AI development, grow continuously, and make a more direct impact — which is why I’m very interested in Asilla.

**Tại sao bạn chọn công ty Asilla chúng tôi?**

I chose Asilla because I’m really impressed by the company’s social-value mindset.

Asilla doesn’t just develop AI for commercial purposes — it’s using behavior recognition technology to prevent accidents and save lives.

Not only commercial AI, but AI that can save lives — and I think that’s really meaningful.

I also like the culture of creativity here, where everyone can share ideas and even turn them into real products. For me, that’s a place where an engineer doesn’t just “follow instructions,” but can actually create and innovate.

In addition, Asilla’s international environment, with its R&D center in Vietnam, gives engineers like me the chance to learn advanced technologies while contributing to global projects.

In short, I chose Asilla because I believe it’s a place where I can grow as an engineer and create real value for society through technology.

**Career path của em là gì? Nếu được nhận thì em làm việc trong công ty bao lâu?**

My long-term goal is to become a Computer Vision specialist within the next 10 years.

I understand this is a long journey that requires both research and real project experience, so I’m willing to start from the basics and take steady steps, even if it means going slower at first.

In the first one or two years, I want to focus on understanding the company’s products and technologies, learn the current pipeline in detail, and take on a hands-on role as a developer — working on coding, model processing, and performance optimization.

At the same time, I plan to pursue advanced courses and certifications in AI, Python, Computer Vision, model training, and optimization, to strengthen my technical foundation.

Regarding my long-term commitment, I truly want to grow together with the company.

I believe that a stable, research-oriented environment like Asilla will allow me to develop steadily and contribute meaningful value to the team over time.

**Mindset của em là gì?**

As for my mindset — during my university years, I worked on some embedded projects that integrated AI, and through those experiences I realized how powerful AI can be in improving human life.

Since I already have a background in embedded systems, I feel that a company like Asilla, where AI and embedded technologies intersect, is the ideal place for me to apply what I know and keep learning deeply about AI.

**Nếu được giao một dự án, bạn sẽ làm gì đầu tiên?**

1. Role PM

First, I would clearly define my role in the project — for example, as a Project Manager, my first priority would be to set up the team structure, assign responsibilities, and evaluate each member’s strengths to ensure the right people are in the right positions.

Next, I would review the project requirements, scope, timeline, and constraints, then build a realistic plan.

Together with the team, I would define the workflow, tools, and development environment.

Once everything is ready, I’d start with the highest-priority tasks and closely monitor progress, quality, and communication among members to keep the project on track.

2. Role Dev

If I were assigned as a developer, the first thing I’d do is study the system requirements carefully — including objectives, main functions, technical constraints, and related documents like specs, design, and API definitions.

If something is unclear, I would discuss it early with my leader or BA to avoid misunderstanding.

Then I’d set up my development environment, prepare repositories, and review the existing codebase if the project is ongoing.

I’d start from the highest-priority modules to ensure stable initial progress.

Throughout the project, I always report progress regularly, discuss problems proactively, and support teammates when needed — because I believe teamwork and smooth communication are the keys to project success.

3. Role SA

As a System Architect, the first thing I’d do is understand the business goals and technical requirements — because a good design only makes sense if it solves the right problem.

Then, I would define the overall system architecture — selecting technologies, frameworks, data flows, and communication mechanisms (API, gRPC, message queue, etc.), and considering non-functional requirements such as performance, scalability, and maintainability.

I would also assess the team’s technical capabilities to choose practical solutions — not necessarily the newest technologies, but those that are reliable and maintainable.

Next, I’d establish coding guidelines, review processes, and CI/CD pipelines to ensure quality and consistency.

Finally, I would support the team during implementation, help solve complex issues, and maintain architectural consistency throughout the project.

4. Common

When I’m join a new project or task, 1st thing I do is understand the team’s goal and my role in that.

Effective work is not only about technical skills, but also clear communication and collaboration.

I usually talk with the leader or experienced members to learn the project’s current stage, priorities, and workflow, then make a small personal plan to adapt quickly with the team.

I always try to communicate proactively, report progress, ask questions early, and help others when needed — because I think trust and teamwork are the foundation of success.

After each sprint or milestone, I like to reflect on what went well and what can be improved, since I believe every project is not just work but also a chance to grow with the team.

**Khi gặp khó khăn, em thường xử lý như thế nào?**

When I face difficulties, the first thing I do is to identify the type of problem — whether it’s technical, human-related, or process-related.

🔹 For technical issues, I usually analyze the root cause using the 5 Whys method, check logs, test hypotheses step by step, and discuss with senior members if needed.

🔹 For human or communication problems, I try to talk directly and calmly, listen to the other person’s perspective, and find a solution that works for both sides.

🔹 For process or schedule issues, I assess the impact and report early to my leader or PM, then work with the team to reprioritize tasks or adjust the plan.

I believe that staying calm, communicating early, and analyzing logically are the best ways to overcome most kinds of difficulties.
