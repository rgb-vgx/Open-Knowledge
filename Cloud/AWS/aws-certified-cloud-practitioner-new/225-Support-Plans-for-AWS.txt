So now let's talk about
the AWS support plans.
So basic support is free,
and then you have three plans,
Business Support+, Enterprise Support,
and Unified Operations.
And there is a minimum spend per plan,
and it gives you a pricing structure based on
how many charges you have and the percentage.
But let's have a look, more importantly,
at what the features are.
So the Basic Support Plan gives you customer service
and communities with 24/7 access to customer service,
documentation, whitepapers, and support forums.
It gives you Trusted Advisor
with seven Trusted Advisor checks
and guidance to provision your resources
following best practices
to increase performance and improve security.
And it gives you access
to the AWS Personal Health Dashboard,
which is a personalized view of the health of your services,
and alerts if your resources are impacted.
Next we have the AWS Business Support+ Plan,
which is intended if you have production workloads.
It gives you real time and contextual responses
through GenerativeAI.
You get Trusted Advisor with a full set of checks
plus API access to Trusted Advisor.
You have 24/7 access to Cloud Support Engineers
through phone, web, and chat.
And you have unlimited cases and unlimited contacts.
You will wait no more than 30 minutes
before getting a human support to respond
to your business-critical system down cases.
And you have third party software support.
That means, for example,
that if your EC2 is not operating Amazon,
but instead Ubuntu,
then the Cloud Support Engineers
can help you with that, as well.
For Enterprise Support Plan now,
this is when you have production
or business critical workloads.
So you get all of the features from the previous plan,
but also you get access
to a designated Technical Account Manager, or TAM.
It will take you less than 15 minutes
for your case response,
in case of a production-critical issue.
And you get access
to the AWS Security Incident Response team,
which helps you recover from security incidents.
You're going to get business reviews from AWS experts
and you get access to AWS Countdown event management,
which are specialized TAM-led support to help you succeed
during the critical business events.
Finally, you get the AWS
Unified Operations Support Plan 24/7,
which is used if you have mission critical workloads.
So you get all of the features
in the Business Support Plan+,
but you also get Application Architecture Guidance
to help you design architectures
that will fit use case and workloads.
It will give you a short-term engagement
with the AWS Support
for deep understanding analysis,
and then provide architectural guidance.
And you get access to a designated TAM,
a Domain Specialist Engineer, a DSE,
a Senior Billing and Account Specialist, SBAS.
You get also the Incident Management Engineer, IME,
a Migration Specialist on-demand,
and a Specialist Support Engineer, SSE.
You also get access to the AWS Countdown Premium
and AWS Customer Incident Response Team, CIRT.
So these are really big, big enterprise
mission critical features.
And you get critical workloads review
and operational procedures.
So that's it, now you've seen
all the different support plans.
So the exam was two easy questions
about which one to choose,
but now you know really the differences between all of them.
So I hope you liked it
and I will see you in the next lecture.
