its responses. So one is called Rouge, and that is a set of metrics for text summarization or
machine translation. And its job is to count the number of overlapping units between the computer

So remember, you know, one way of evaluating these models is to take its output and compare it to

So how similar is that generated result to the ideal result in terms of words or n-grams or
fragments of sentences? One specific type of Rouge is Rouge-n, and that's measuring the level of
overlap on n-grams, which is n is just how many words at a time am I comparing to each other.
I'll give you an example in a second here. So Rouge-1 would be based on the overlap between
unigrams, which are just one word at a time. Rouge-2 would be based on overlap of bigrams,
