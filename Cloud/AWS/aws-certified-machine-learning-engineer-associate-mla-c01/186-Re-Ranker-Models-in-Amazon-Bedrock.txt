Let's quickly talk about another tool in your tool chest
for improving the quality
of your retrieval augmented generation context
that's retrieved from your vector stores.
That's re-ranker models used with Amazon Bedrock.
So that attempts to improve the relevance
of the retrieved results from your underlying vector store,
from your knowledge base.
Now, the problem is that the chunks you got back
might not necessarily be all that relevant
to the original query.
And this is a way of making sure that it is.
So the idea here is that we're not just looking
at the vector distances, the actual embeddings,
the semantic search results from that information
that we're retrieving for context.
We're also gonna look at the relevance
of those individual chunks to the original query.
And then we're gonna reorder those results accordingly
to choose what the best ones are.
So it's not just about semantic search in this case,
we're using that as a starting point
and then using the relevance of those chunks
to the original query to try to re-rank them again
in order to get the best result at the top somehow.
How do you do that?
Well, there's a re-rank operation in the Bedrock API
that you can use to do that and trigger that re-ranking
whenever you want relative to a query to your Bedrock model.
And you can also specify a re-ranker model explicitly
when hitting your knowledge base at that level as well.
Now, there are built-in ones you can use.
All it has to do is measure that relevance somehow
from the chunk to the query, right?
It's not that complicated of a problem to describe.
There are two different models
you can choose for that problem.
There are from Amazon and currently from Cohere.
However, they are only available in limited regions.
So do double check that re-ranking is available
in the region where you've deployed Bedrock
before trying to use it.
