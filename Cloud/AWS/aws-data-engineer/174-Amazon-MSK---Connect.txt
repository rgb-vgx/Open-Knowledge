Narrator: So here is a feature
about MSK called MSK Connect.
The idea is that in the Kafka world,
you have something called Kafka Connect,
and Kafka Connect is a framework to take data from Kafka
and put it somewhere else,
or (indistinct) to take data from somewhere else
and put it in Kafka.
And when you use a Kafka Connect framework in MSK,
what you can do is to use MSK Connect, which is going
to give you a managed Kafka Connect workers on AWS
with auto-scaling capabilities.
That means that you can deploy the infrastructure
of Kafka Connect on MSK Connect,
and then you can deploy any Kafka Connect connector
to the MSK Connect as a plugin.
And so these plugins can be, for example,
sending data into Amazon S3, Amazon Redshift,
Amazon OpenSearch, Debezium, and so on.
And so here's an example.
You have the MSK cluster up and running.
Then you're going
to create your MSK Connect workers,
thanks to the MSK Connect service,
and you will deploy the plugin,
being the Amazon S3 Kafka connector.
And then the Connect workers are going
to pull the topic data from your MSK cluster,
and then the data will be written to Amazon S3 as a result.
And we haven't managed any infrastructure.
It's all done by MSK Connect
and it is just configuration on our end.
So there's a pricing.
Here's an example.
You pay 11 cents per worker per hour.
And of course, the more data you have,
the more workers you're going to need.
So that's it for MSK Connect.
Just introduction in case you see it in the exam.
I hope you liked it and I will see you in the next lecture.
