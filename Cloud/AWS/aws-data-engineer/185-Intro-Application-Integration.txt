-: Application integration is a very important piece
of creating a data engineering pipeline.
There's not a crazy amount of information you need
to study here, but it's very important to internalize
how Amazon Event Bridge, Amazon App Flow
and AWS step functions fit into your data pipelines
and automating your ETL flow.
Stefan will also cover Amazon's Simple Queue service
and Amazon's Simple Notification service as part
of this section, and I'll pop in to discuss a newer system,
Amazon Managed Workflows for Apache Airflow
or Amazon MWAA for short.
It's part of what seems like a trend
of Amazon offering fully managed environments
for open source tools
with some extra AWS integration added in.
When you're done, you'll practice applying these tools
to real-world problems with a short quiz.
So let's dive in.
