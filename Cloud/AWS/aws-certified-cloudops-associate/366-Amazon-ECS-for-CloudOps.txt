Here are extra bits of knowledge you may need
on ECS for the CloudOps exam.
So the first thing is that you have ephemeral storage
for Fargate tasks.
That means that you have storage that can be shared
amongst other tasks in your Fargate containers,
and you can configure this from 20 gigabytes
to a maximum of 200 gigabytes.
And that storage is encrypted.
It's ephemeral, meaning that once your task is over,
the storage will disappear.
You also can send ECS task log to CloudWatch
or a Partner using what's called the FireLens,
which is developed by Fluent Bit or Fluentd,
container to your ECS task definition.
It's called a sidecar pattern
because it runs as a new container,
as a new task alongside your other containers,
and is going to act as a log router
and send the log directly into CloudWatch or elsewhere.
So that's it for this lecture.
I hope you liked it, and I will see you in the next lecture.
