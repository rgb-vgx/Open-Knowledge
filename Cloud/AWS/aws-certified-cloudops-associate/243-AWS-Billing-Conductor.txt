So now let's talk about AWS Billing Conductor.
So it's a service that's used for customizing
and presenting your AWS billing data.
So the idea is that it doesn't change your bill,
but you can group accounts,
you can apply markups and discounts,
you can generate pro forma bills,
and this is all things that actually are needed
maybe by your company.
So this doesn't affect your bill,
it just affects how it's displayed and how it's allocated.
So common use cases is for enterprises to split bill
by department or cost centers,
for MSPs to apply custom pricing per customer,
for finance team to build chargeback or showback models,
and for subsidiary
to create a transparent internal invoice system.
So it's helpful for big organizations
with big accounting needs.
Definitely not helpful for small organizations,
but it's good to know that you can actually modify your bill
to make it fit the way you do accounting,
and that's using AWS Billing Conductor.
